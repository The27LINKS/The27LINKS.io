Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026.

Plain-English version: files processed by our browser-based tools stay in your browser, but account-based services, analytics, advertising and external providers may process information as described in this policy.

This Privacy Policy explains how The27LINKS ("we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, stores and discloses information when you visit the27links.in or use our website tools, learning services and certification features (collectively, the "Service").

This policy applies to our browser-based tools, website accounts, Google Sign-In, course enrollment, learning records, certificates, contact channels and related website features. Not every feature is necessarily available on every page.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Information processed by browser-based tools

Many of our tools, including PDF utilities, image compression, QR generation and related browser-based features, are designed to process the files or text you select directly in your browser. For tools that operate entirely client-side, the working files are not uploaded to our application server as part of the processing operation.

This statement applies only to tools that actually perform processing locally. It does not mean that every interaction with the website is free of network requests. Website hosting, fonts, analytics, advertising, external resources and account services may communicate with their respective providers.

1.2 Information you provide

When you contact us through email or a contact form, we may receive your name, email address, message and any other information you choose to provide. We use this information to respond to enquiries, provide support and address reported issues.

1.3 Google Sign-In and account information

If you choose to sign in using Google, Google authenticates your account and provides the account information permitted by the OAuth sign-in process and the permissions you approve.

Depending on the configured integration, this may include your Google account identifier, name, email address and profile image. We use the information made available to us to authenticate you, identify your account, create or access your student profile and provide the account features you request.

We do not receive your Google account password through Google Sign-In. We do not request access to Google Drive, Gmail contents, contacts or other Google data unless a separate feature explicitly requires it and appropriate authorization is obtained.

1.4 Student, course and certificate information

When you use our learning or certification services, we may process and store the following information, depending on which features you use:

  • Student account identifier and profile name.
  • Email address associated with the authenticated account.
  • Course identifiers, course enrollment and enrollment dates.
  • Lesson completion, learning progress and assessment results.
  • Certificate number, course name, issue date and validity status.
  • Records needed to investigate support requests or account issues.

These records allow us to associate learning activity and certificates with the correct account, display course progress, administer assessments and support certificate verification. We collect only the information needed for the features actually provided.

1.5 Technical and usage information

Depending on the services enabled, technical information may be processed by our hosting, authentication, analytics and security providers. This can include IP address, browser and device information, timestamps, referring pages, diagnostic events and information about interactions with the website.

Google Analytics may process usage and event information according to our configuration and Google's documentation. Hosting or security services may also generate technical logs to operate, maintain and protect the Service.

2. How we use information

We use relevant information for the following purposes:

  • To operate and maintain the website and its tools.
  • To authenticate users and maintain secure login sessions.
  • To create and manage student accounts and profiles.
  • To provide course enrollment and learning functionality.
  • To record course progress and assessment outcomes.
  • To issue, maintain, verify or revoke certificates.
  • To respond to enquiries, feedback and support requests.
  • To diagnose technical problems and protect against abuse.
  • To understand website usage and improve the Service.
  • To deliver and measure advertising where advertising is enabled.
  • To meet applicable legal obligations and protect legitimate rights.

3. Google API Services and Google user data

Google Sign-In is used to authenticate users and obtain the account information permitted by the configured OAuth integration. We use that information for sign-in, account identification and the learning or certification features requested by the user.

Google user data is not sold. We do not use Google user data for targeted advertising, nor do we use it to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

We do not transfer Google user data to third parties except as necessary to provide the user-requested service, comply with applicable law, or as otherwise permitted by the applicable Google API Services User Data Policy and the user's authorization.

Any handling or transfer of Google user data is subject to the applicable Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements where applicable. We do not claim access to Google data or permissions that our actual integration does not request.

Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy. Information about Google API data requirements is available in the Google API Services User Data Policy .

4. Database storage and service providers

Our public website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Where the account and certification features are enabled, we use Supabase for authentication and database functionality. Google provides the Google Sign-In service.

Information necessary for accounts, profiles, enrollments and certificates may be stored in a Supabase-managed database. Authentication services process information to establish user identity and maintain sessions. Database access is intended to be restricted according to account permissions and applicable security policies.

We may rely on service providers for website hosting, authentication, database infrastructure, analytics, advertising, email or support. These providers may process information as needed to deliver their services, subject to their applicable terms, policies and contractual arrangements.

Relevant provider documentation:

  • Supabase Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Privacy Notice
  • Google Privacy Policy

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell personal information or Google user data. We may disclose information in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers: to providers supporting hosting, authentication, databases, analytics, advertising, security and support, where needed for their functions.
  • Legal requirements: where disclosure is required by applicable law or a valid legal process.
  • Security: where reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, protect users or safeguard the Service.
  • With your direction: where you request a disclosure or authorize a specific integration.

We do not permit service providers to use Google user data for purposes prohibited by the applicable Google API Services User Data Policy.

6. Cookies, authentication sessions and advertising

Our website and its providers may use cookies, browser storage, session tokens and similar technologies to support login sessions, maintain authentication state, protect accounts, measure usage and deliver website functionality.

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to measure website activity. If Google AdSense or other advertising services are enabled, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure or personalize advertisements, subject to applicable settings, consent requirements and regional rules.

Where required, we will provide appropriate consent and preference controls before using non-essential cookies or similar technologies. Available controls may depend on your region and the services enabled on the website.

For more information, see our Cookie Policy and Google's advertising information.

7. Public certificate verification

Where public certificate verification is available, a visitor may enter a certificate number to check whether a certificate exists and whether it remains valid.

Depending on the verification feature, the response may display limited information such as the certificate holder's display name, course title, certificate number, issue date and validity or revocation status. This is intended to help verify educational credentials.

Public verification should not disclose passwords, authentication tokens, private account credentials, student email addresses or unrelated private profile information. We restrict the information intended for public display to what is needed for verification.

8. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including providing the Service, maintaining account functionality, resolving support requests, protecting security and meeting applicable legal obligations.

  • Contact messages: retained as needed to respond to requests, maintain relevant records and meet applicable obligations.
  • Account information: retained while needed to provide and manage the account and its associated services.
  • Learning records: retained as needed to maintain enrollment, progress and assessment functionality.
  • Certificate records: may be retained after course completion to support credential verification, prevent misuse and maintain accurate issuance records.
  • Technical and analytics records: retained according to the relevant provider settings, operational requirements and applicable policies.

Retention periods may vary by record type. When information is no longer needed, we will take appropriate steps to delete or de-identify it, subject to applicable law and legitimate recordkeeping requirements.

9. Access, correction and account deletion

You may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information associated with your account, subject to applicable law and reasonable identity verification.

To request deletion of a learning account, contact us using the email address listed in Section 14. Please identify the account using the email address associated with it and describe the request. Do not send your password or authentication tokens.

Where appropriate, we will delete or de-identify the associated account profile, enrollment records and other personal information that we control. Certain records may need to be retained where required by law or reasonably necessary for security, dispute resolution or legitimate certificate verification. We will explain applicable limitations where required.

Signing out of Google does not delete the account or the information already stored by our Service. Revoking Google access also does not automatically remove records from our database. Contact us separately to request account deletion.

You may also manage or revoke third-party account access through the relevant provider's account settings. This does not replace a deletion request to us.

10. Security

We use HTTPS for website communication and rely on the security features of our hosting and authentication services. Account-based services should use authentication checks, restricted database access and appropriate access-control policies to protect student records.

We aim to limit the collection and disclosure of personal information to what is necessary for the Service. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

11. Children's privacy

Our website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. Where courses or certification services are made available to minors, we will apply the consent and data-protection requirements that apply to the relevant users and jurisdiction.

If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

12. International processing

Our hosting, authentication, database, analytics and other service providers may process information in countries outside your country of residence. Where applicable, such processing is subject to relevant provider terms and appropriate safeguards required by applicable law.

13. Your privacy rights and policy changes

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain information about the processing of your personal data, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You may contact us to exercise applicable rights.

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, data practices or legal obligations change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent for material changes.
