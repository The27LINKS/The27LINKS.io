About the Free Online PDF Splitter

Have a massive PDF document but only need a few specific pages? The The27LINKS PDF Split tool makes it incredibly easy to extract exactly what you need. Whether you want to pull a single invoice out of a monthly statement, or divide a 100-page eBook into individual chapters, you can do it here instantly.

100% Client-Side Privacy: We know PDFs often contain highly sensitive data—from tax returns to legal contracts. Unlike other services that upload your files to a cloud server to process them, our tool utilizes modern browser technology to split your PDFs entirely locally. Your files never leave your computer, ensuring absolute privacy.

How to Split Your PDF

  • Extract Specific Pages: Select the "Extract specific pages" mode. In the input box, type the exact pages you want. You can use commas for individual pages and hyphens for ranges (e.g., 1, 4, 8-12). The tool will combine just those pages into one new, smaller PDF file.
  • Split Every Page: Select the "Split every page" mode. This will instantly break apart your document, downloading every single page as its own individual PDF file (e.g., file-page-1.pdf, file-page-2.pdf). Note: Your browser may ask for permission to download multiple files at once.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will splitting the PDF reduce its quality?

No, not at all. The tool performs a native PDF extraction. It does not rasterize or convert your pages into images, meaning text remains selectable and vector graphics remain perfectly sharp.

Is there a limit to how many pages I can split?

There are no strict limits because the processing uses your device's hardware. However, if you attempt to use the "Split every page" mode on a 1,000-page document, your browser will attempt to initiate 1,000 separate downloads, which may cause it to freeze. For massive documents, we recommend using the range extractor instead.
