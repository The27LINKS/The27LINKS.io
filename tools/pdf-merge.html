About the Free Online PDF Merger

Whether you are compiling monthly reports, combining scanned receipts for expenses, or joining study notes, the The27LINKS PDF Merge tool makes it effortless. Combine unlimited PDF files into one unified, easily shareable document in mere seconds without needing to download expensive software.

100% Client-Side Privacy: We understand that your PDFs contain highly sensitive data—from tax returns to legal contracts. Unlike other services that upload your files to a cloud server to merge them, our tool utilizes modern browser technology to merge your PDFs entirely locally. Your files never leave your computer, ensuring absolute privacy and data security.

How to Combine PDF Files

  • Upload Your Files: Drag and drop multiple PDF files into the upload box, or click to select them from your computer.
  • Arrange the Order: The files will merge in the exact order they appear in the list. To change the order, simply click and drag a file's row up or down to reposition it.
  • Merge & Download: Click the "Merge & download" button. The tool will instantly stitch the documents together and trigger a download of your new, unified PDF.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a limit to how many files I can merge?

There are no hard limits on the number of files you can upload. However, because the merging process relies on your device's available memory (RAM), attempting to merge hundreds of extremely large files at once may cause older devices or browsers to lag.

Will merging reduce the quality of my PDFs?

No. The merger performs a native binary join of the PDF pages. It does not rasterize, compress, or convert your pages into images, meaning text remains selectable, vector graphics stay sharp, and original quality is perfectly preserved.
