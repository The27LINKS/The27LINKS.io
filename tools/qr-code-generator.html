About the Free QR Code Generator

Quick Response (QR) codes are the easiest way to connect the physical world to the digital one. The The27LINKS QR Code Generator allows you to instantly create scannable codes for your website, contact information, or Wi-Fi network. Unlike many other online generators that charge fees or expire your links, our tool creates static, permanent QR codes completely for free.

100% Private & Secure: All QR codes are generated locally inside your web browser. This means your private Wi-Fi passwords, personal phone numbers, and emails are never sent to or stored on our servers.

What Can You Create?

  • Plain Text & URLs: Paste a link to your website, YouTube channel, or digital menu. When scanned, it instantly opens the link in the user's browser.
  • Wi-Fi Networks: Tired of spelling out your complex Wi-Fi password for guests? Enter your SSID and password here. When guests scan the printed code, their phones will automatically connect to your network without typing a thing.
  • Contact Cards (vCard): Generate a digital business card. By scanning the code, your name, phone number, email, and company will instantly be added to the scanner's phone contacts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will my QR Code ever expire?

No! The QR codes generated by this tool are "Static." They contain the actual data (like the URL or text) hardcoded into the pattern. Because they don't rely on redirect links, they will work forever.

Can I change the color of the QR code?

Yes, you can use the "Foreground color" picker to match your brand. However, please note that QR scanners rely on high contrast. Always use a dark color for the QR code against a light or white background, otherwise, phone cameras might fail to read it.
