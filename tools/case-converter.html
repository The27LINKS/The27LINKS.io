About the Case Converter

The27LINKS Case Converter is a free utility designed to instantly fix capitalization errors and format text for coding, writing, and data entry. Because this tool runs entirely in your browser using local JavaScript, your text remains private and is never uploaded to any servers.

Formatting Options Explained

  • Sentence case: Capitalizes the first letter of a sentence.
  • UPPERCASE & lowercase: Converts all characters uniformly.
  • Title Case: Capitalizes the first letter of every word, ideal for blog post titles.
  • camelCase & PascalCase: Removes spaces and capitalizes words. Standard in JavaScript and object-oriented programming.
  • snake_case: Replaces spaces with underscores. Popular in Python and database architectures.
  • kebab-case: Replaces spaces with hyphens. The standard format for URLs and SEO-friendly file naming.
